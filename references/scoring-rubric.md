# Scoring rubric

Score fit from 0 to 100 as the sum of four parts. Use the bands, not false precision.

| Part | Points | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Responsibilities | 40 | Do the day-to-day tasks in the posting match work the profile shows the user has done? |
| Requirements | 30 | Are the stated skills and tools covered by evidence in the profile? |
| Level | 20 | Does the seniority, scope and years requested fit the user? |
| Context | 10 | Does the domain or industry connect to anything in the profile? |

## How to score each part
- **Responsibilities (40).** List the three to five main tasks in the posting. For each, find profile evidence.
  - 32 to 40: most tasks are covered by evidence.
  - 20 to 31: about half are covered, the rest are adjacent.
  - 8 to 19: a few are adjacent, most are new.
  - 0 to 7: almost none relate.
- **Requirements (30).** Separate "required" from "preferred". Missing a required item costs more than missing a preferred item.
  - 24 to 30: all required items covered.
  - 15 to 23: one or two required items missing or only partly covered.
  - 6 to 14: several required items missing.
  - 0 to 5: the core requirement is missing.
- **Level (20).** Compare the years, team size and scope with the profile.
  - 16 to 20: matches the user's level.
  - 8 to 15: one step above or below.
  - 0 to 7: a clear mismatch (for example "10+ years" for a new graduate).
- **Context (10).** 7 to 10 for a direct domain link, 3 to 6 for an adjacent one, 0 to 2 for none. Do not reward a famous company name.

## Hard gates
Check these before the score. Mark each pass, fail or unknown.
- Location
- Work authorization (visa, citizenship)
- Language the posting requires
- Hard experience requirement (a stated minimum the user does not meet)

A failed gate moves the posting to the dropped list with the reason, and the score is still shown. An unknown gate stays on the list with a note: "not stated, ask the employer". Do not guess.

## Confidence
Mark confidence high, medium or low next to the score.
- Low when the posting is short, vague or mostly boilerplate.
- Medium when the main tasks are clear but requirements are partly missing.
- High when tasks, requirements and level are all stated.

## Priority (separate from fit)
After scoring, order postings that have similar fit (within about 10 points) by:
1. How recently the posting went up, if the date is known. Unknown dates sort last.
2. The user's stated preferences, such as company type.

Preferences and freshness never add points to the fit score. If a preferred company has a lower fit, it stays lower.

## Evidence rule
Every score needs two quotes: one line from the posting and one line from the profile. If you cannot find a profile line, the part scores in the lowest band for that item and you say it is a gap.
